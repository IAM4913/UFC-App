How to use during the draft

  1. Set Teams, My pick, roster and scoring in Settings (or paste your Yahoo League Settings page).
  2. Every time someone drafts, type the name in the search box and press Enter. The pick goes to the team on the clock and the board advances. Use Assign pick to only if Yahoo's order differs (keepers).
  3. When it is your turn the top bar pulses. The Advice panel shows the best picks with reasons; the table's Score column ranks everyone.
  4. Ctrl+Z undoes the last pick. Click any board cell to remove that pick.
  5. Fell behind? Sync → paste the Yahoo Draft Results text and every missing pick is applied at once.

How the numbers work

  • Proj: 2026 season projection scored with your league's settings.
  • VORP: points over the replacement-level player at that position (starters + flex share + a little bench), recomputed from who is left on the board.
  • VONA: points over the best player at that position you can expect to still be available at your next pick (ADP-based probabilities).
  • Avail: chance the player is still on the board at your next pick.
  • Score = need × (0.55 VORP + 0.45 VONA). Need drops for positions you have already filled, and kickers/defenses are held back until the last rounds.
